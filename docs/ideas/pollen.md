# pollen, reaching the people a product is for

2026-10-26. A draft, not a loop. Layer: marketing (`docs/decisions/2026-10-26-layers.md`). The
name passes to this bed when the messaging tool becomes mycelium (`docs/plans/mycelium-rename.md`).
Pollen leaves one flower and starts new growth in many others.

## Who it is for, and their pain

An owner who can build a product and has no time or skill to tell the people who need it that it
exists. Marketing done by hand is either skipped or done in bursts, with claims nobody checked,
spend nobody tracked and no record of which move brought anyone in.

## What it owns, and what it does not

pollen owns reaching people who have not heard of the product yet: positioning, messaging,
landing pages and store listings, launch posts, content and search, ads, and outreach. It does not
speak to people who already asked or already use the product (harvest does), choose the product's
features (graft does) or set its voice (petals does).

## How it works

1. **Position.** From cultivate's jobs and outcomes, in users' own words, and petals' voice:
   who the product is for, the job it does, what it replaces.
2. **Plan moves.** Each move is one file: audience, channel, message, the claims it makes, budget,
   tracked link, kill line, and whether it may run alone.
3. **Check.** Every claim points at a shipped feature in graft's map, so nothing promises what
   does not exist. Copy passes petals' brand check. Spend is reserved in well. hedge refuses every
   post and send that lacks a yes the compass requires.
4. **Run.** On the owner's own surfaces and within a reserved budget, a move runs alone after one
   yes per campaign. Where a platform forbids automated posting, and for any outreach to people
   who did not ask, pollen drafts and queues the move for a person.
5. **Measure.** Each move's tracked link and analytics say whether it brought people in. A move
   below its kill line stops.

Before a product is built, a landing page tests whether anyone wants it. The signups go back to
cultivate as evidence, and to glean's score.

## What code decides

The claim-to-feature check, the brand check, the budget, the yes, the platform rules per channel,
the kill line per move. A person or a drafting agent writes the copy; code decides whether it may
go out.

## Alone, and with the garden

Alone, it is a plan of marketing moves with tracked results and a budget, for any product. With
the garden, its claims are checked against graft's map and its spend against well.

## Data and its lifetime

Move files and their results are kept for the life of the product. pollen keeps no contact list.
Signup addresses belong to the product and follow its data lifetime (trellis).

## Metric and kill criterion

Metric: cost per signup per move, against each move's kill line.

Kill criterion: if product one's moves cannot be measured one by one through tracked links,
pollen is a drafting skill, not a tool.

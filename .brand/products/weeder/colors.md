# weeder, colour delta

Inherits the full plotplot palette. One product claim:

- **Bloom `#A37A6E`**, the deepest built grey of the shed (wood and iron), from the umbrella
  Product Accents table. weeder uses it for its mark's tile, the bloom dot in the garden row, rule
  ids and the `[weeder]` prefix, and the block-level marker in its table output. Where it must
  read as a word on paper, use weeder-word (`--pp-weeder-word`, 4.5:1 on paper and on soil-night).
- Status stays umbrella: **healthy `#46913C`**, caution `#B0741C`, **error `#BC4126`**. The bloom
  marks *weeder*; error marks a *block-level result*; caution marks a *warning*; healthy marks
  *clean*. Never set a finding in the bloom to make it look serious.

No other colours are added. Measured values live in the umbrella's `colors.md`, computed by
`scripts/palette_contrast.py`.

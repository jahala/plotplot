## Positioning

Peer messaging for agents. umbel fans workers out from one stem and pleach conducts a plan
through gates; both speak down the tree. mycelium is the network beneath: one agent asks
another and uses the answer, without a supervisor relaying it. A stranger knocks, and a person
decides who is let in.

## Mark

Two plants of different heights rise from one hidden thread, with a knot on the thread between
them. The stems and leaves are growth green (`#357E2C`); the thread and the knot are heather
(`#7462B8`). The reading is the product: separate growers, joined below the surface, where what
passes between them travels. It keeps the garden's diagram language, stroked stems and filled
leaves, and sits apart from tilth's mark, whose single seedling stands over a straight ground
line and a root tree.
